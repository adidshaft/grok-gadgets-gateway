# Code of conduct

The canonical [Grok Gadgets policy](https://github.com/adidshaft/grok-gadgets/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)
applies to gateway issues, pull requests, reviews, and project spaces. The link is a planned
destination until approved activation; these rules also apply during local preparation.

Be respectful, constructive, and inclusive. Critique behavior and code, not people.
Harassment, threats, discrimination, unwanted contact, and disclosure of someone else's
private information are unacceptable. Respect stated boundaries and reviewers' time.

Report concerns privately to **adidshaft@kyokasuigetsu.xyz**. **@adidshaft** is initially
responsible; no additional moderation team is implied. Share only what is needed to investigate.
Responses may include correction, warning, temporary restriction, or removal, proportionate
to conduct and repetition. Appeal through the same email with the decision and context;
the maintainer will reconsider it. No confidentiality guarantee or response SLA is invented.
